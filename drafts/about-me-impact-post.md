# LinkedIn Post Draft — About Me: Participant-Led Impact

---

**Draft A — Story-led (recommended)**

Most support handovers start the same way.

A new worker arrives. The participant — or their family — has to explain everything again.

The diagnosis. The communication style. The triggers. What a good day looks like. What makes things worse. The food preferences. The routine. The things that seem small but aren't.

Every. Single. Time.

That's not a support model. That's a burden placed on the people who already carry the most.

---

The About Me Profile at InSync Profiles was built to fix that.

It's a free, participant-led digital profile — created by the person themselves, or with their family or carer — that travels with them across every support interaction.

Here's what it actually does:

**It gives participants authorship.**
They decide what's in it. Their words. Their framing. Not a clinician's summary. Not a case note. Their voice.

**It carries the support model forward.**
Communication style, AAC device, yes/no signals, distress signals, what helps, what makes things worse — all of it is documented once and shared with every worker who needs it.

**It reduces the re-explanation burden.**
No more starting from scratch with every new worker, every agency, every shift change. The profile does the introducing.

**It protects dignity in vulnerable moments.**
When someone is distressed, the last thing they need is a worker who doesn't know them. The About Me profile means workers arrive informed — not guessing.

**It supports consistency, not just compliance.**
A support plan tells workers what to do. The About Me profile tells them who they're supporting. That's the difference between task completion and genuine connection.

**It's built for accessibility from the ground up.**
Read Aloud, dyslexia font, high contrast, large text, AAC communication board — because the people who need this most are often the people most excluded by standard digital tools.

**It's free. Always.**
Because access to good support shouldn't depend on a budget line.
